I don't hear anything on the hour

Check three things: ChimeTime is switched on from the menu bar, the current hour isn't inside your Quiet Hours, and your Mac isn't muted. You can confirm the sound itself with the Preview button in Settings.

How do I stop it chiming at night?

Open Settings and set your Quiet Hours. ChimeTime stays silent during those hours automatically. This is free — you never need Pro to silence it.

What's free and what's Pro?

The hourly chime, Speak Time, Quiet Hours, Launch at Login, and the on/off toggle are free forever. ChimeTime Pro is an optional one-time purchase that unlocks custom sounds, appearance, a per-hour schedule, Pomodoro, a global shortcut, the half-hour chime, chime count, a menu bar clock, chime history, multi-monitor, and Calendar Quiet Hours.

I bought Pro but it's locked on another Mac

Open the Pro screen (or the About tab) and choose Restore Purchases. As long as you're signed into the same Apple ID, Pro unlocks. It's a one-time purchase — never a subscription.

Does ChimeTime collect my data?

No. Nothing is collected, tracked, or sent anywhere. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Which macOS versions are supported?

macOS 13 (Ventura) and later.
