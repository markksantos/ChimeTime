ChimeTime Privacy Policy

Last updated: [DATE]

ChimeTime collects nothing. There is no account, no analytics, and no tracking. Everything the app does happens on your Mac.

What we collect

Nothing. ChimeTime does not gather, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. There is no sign-in and no user profile.

Where your settings live

Your preferences — chime sound, schedule, quiet hours, and so on — are stored locally on your Mac using the standard macOS settings store and the app's own sandbox container. They never leave your device. Custom sound files you add are copied into that same local container.

Network use

ChimeTime makes exactly one kind of network request: to Apple's App Store, to verify a ChimeTime Pro purchase and restore it across your devices. This is handled entirely by Apple's StoreKit. We never see your payment details, and no purchase information is sent to us or to any third party.

Calendar access (optional)

If you turn on Calendar Quiet Hours, ChimeTime asks macOS for read-only access to your calendar so it can stay silent during events. It reads only whether a given time is busy, on your Mac. No calendar data is stored, transmitted, or shared. If you leave this feature off, ChimeTime never touches your calendar.

Children

ChimeTime is safe for all ages. Because it collects no data, it collects none from children.
